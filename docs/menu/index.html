    Block 05 The icon and its menu

    Reading time · 3 min

    Where the icon is, what its colours mean, and what each item in its menu does.

    In this block

    1. Where the icon is
    2. What the icon tells you
    3. The menu, item by item
    4. The keys shown in the menu
    5. Quitting

    Open Whisper has no main window: an icon is all you see of it, and its menu reaches everything else. This page covers where the icon is, what it tells you, and every item in the menu.

    Where the icon is

    Mac

    In the menu bar, at the top right, near the clock. Open Whisper has no icon in the Dock.

    Windows

    In the notification area, next to the clock. If you do not see it, click ^ and drag the Open Whisper icon onto the taskbar.

    Click the icon to open its menu.

    Only one copy of Open Whisper runs at a time. Opening it again while it is running leaves the running copy in charge: no second icon appears.

    What the icon tells you

    StateThe icon
    ReadyThe plain icon. On the Mac it takes the colour of the other menu-bar icons; on Windows it is white on a dark taskbar and black on a light one.
    RecordingRed.
    Transcribing and pastingPulses. It stays still if motion is reduced in the system (Mac: Accessibility → Reduce motion; Windows: Animation effects off).
    ErrorAmber, for a moment, then back to ready. A notification says what happened.

    A video transcription running in the background does not change the icon: the menu shows its progress instead. Screen readers read the menu's first line, such as Open Whisper — Ready, as the icon's name.

    The menu, item by item

    From top to bottom, with lines separating the groups:

    ItemWhat it does
    Open Whisper — ReadyThe status line. It cannot be clicked. It also reads Recording…, Transcribing…, Delivering… (pasting the text), Error or Recording cancelled.
    Start RecordingStarts a dictation, as the shortcut does, and becomes Stop Recording while it records. It works like Toggle in both modes. Shows your shortcut beside it, and is available only when Open Whisper is ready or recording.
    Copy Last TranscriptionPuts the last dictation's text on the clipboard. See Where the text lands.
    Retry Last TranscriptionPastes the last dictation's text again, without transcribing it again. See Where the text lands.
    Enable auto-paste…Mac only: opens System Settings at Accessibility, to turn auto-paste on.
    Transcribe YouTube Video…Opens the video window, for a YouTube link or a video or audio file on your computer. While a video is being transcribed, it shows the progress, such as (42%). See Video and files.
    Settings…Opens Settings.
    About Open WhisperOpens the About window, with the version number. See About, versions and terms.
    Quit Open WhisperQuits the app (see Quitting).

    Mac

    Once Accessibility is on, Enable auto-paste… turns into Auto-paste is on, which cannot be clicked. If you switched it on and it still says Enable auto-paste…, quit Open Whisper and open it again.

    Windows

    Windows needs no permission for auto-paste, so the menu has no such item.

    The keys shown in the menu

    The menu shows keys beside some items: your dictation shortcut beside Start Recording, ⇧⌘C beside Copy Last Transcription, ⌘, beside Settings… and ⌘Q beside Quit Open Whisper. On Windows they are CtrlShiftC, Ctrl, and CtrlQ.

    They are not shortcuts that work from any app. Outside the menu, only your dictation shortcut reaches Open Whisper, plus Esc while it is recording. All the keys are listed in Shortcuts and keys.

    Quitting

    Choose Quit Open Whisper. The shortcut stops working until you open the app again, and the last dictation, kept only in memory, is forgotten.

    If a video is being transcribed, Open Whisper asks first: A video transcription is still running. Quitting now will lose it. Choose Keep running to go on, or Quit anyway to quit and lose that transcription.

    To have Open Whisper start by itself when you log in, turn on Open at login in Settings.
