    Appendix A2 Shortcuts and keys

    Reading time · 3 min

    Every key Open Whisper uses, on Mac and Windows: the dictation shortcut, Esc, and the keys inside each window.

    In this block

    1. The dictation shortcut
    2. Cancelling a recording
    3. Combinations you can't use
    4. In Settings and setup
    5. Closing a window
    6. In the video window
    7. In the menu

    Open Whisper is fully usable from the keyboard. These are all the keys it uses, on each platform. Only two work from any app: the dictation shortcut and, while you record, Esc.

    The dictation shortcut

    MacWindows
    Default⇧⌘SpaceCtrlShiftSpace
    Used instead when your choice is reserved by the system⇧⌘DCtrlShiftD
    Hold to TalkHold the keys, speak, releaseHold the keys, speak, release
    TogglePress to start, press again to stopPress to start, press again to stop

    The shortcut works with no app in focus, and needs no permission. Change it during setup or in Settings. The key shown beside Start Recording in the menu is always the one really active.

    Cancelling a recording

    Esc is taken only while Open Whisper is recording. The rest of the time it reaches your apps untouched. Once the recording becomes Transcribing, Esc no longer cancels.

    ModeMacWindows
    ToggleEscEsc
    Hold to TalkEsc, or the held keys plus Esc, such as ⇧⌘EscThe held keys plus Esc, such as CtrlAltEsc for CtrlAltSpace

    Windows

    With the default CtrlShiftSpace in Hold to Talk, Esc can't cancel: CtrlShiftEsc is Task Manager. The same goes for a shortcut made of Ctrl alone or Alt alone plus a key (CtrlEsc opens Start, AltEsc switches windows). Release the keys instead, use Toggle mode, or choose a shortcut such as CtrlAltSpace.

    Mac

    A shortcut that holds both ⌥ and ⌘ can't cancel with the held keys: ⌥⌘Esc is Force Quit. Use Toggle mode, or a shortcut without that pair.

    Combinations you can't use

    A shortcut needs a modifier: ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥ on the Mac, Ctrl or Alt on Windows. Shift alone doesn't count. On Windows the Windows key can't be part of it.

    These belong to the system. If you choose one, Settings says it is reserved and the fallback above is used.

    Reserved
    Mac⌘Space ⌃Space ⌃⌥Space ⌘Tab ⇧⌘3 ⇧⌘4 ⇧⌘5 ⌘Q ⌘W
    WindowsCtrlAltDel CtrlShiftEsc CtrlEsc CtrlSpace AltTab AltShiftTab AltF4 AltSpace AltEsc

    A combination another app already uses can't be registered either: Settings says so, and you pick another.

    In Settings and setup

    KeyWhat it does
    TabMoves between the controls
    Space or Enter on the shortcutStarts recording a new shortcut; then press the combination
    Esc while recording a shortcutKeeps the previous shortcut
    Delete or Backspace while recording a shortcutKeeps the previous shortcut
    Arrow keys on ModeSwitches between Hold to Talk and Toggle
    ↑ and ↓ on Stop after silenceAdds or removes half a second
    Arrow keys on the setup stepsMoves to the previous or next step

    Changes in Settings apply at once. There is no Save button.

    Closing a window

    MacWindows
    Close Settings, setup, About or the video windowEsc, ⌘. or ⌘WEsc or CtrlW
    • Closing setup before Done brings it back the next time Open Whisper starts.
    • The video window asks first when a transcript hasn't been copied or saved. The transcript is still there when you reopen it.
